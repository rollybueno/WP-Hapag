Hapag is a place for people who love food but don’t need it to be fussy.

We started with one simple belief: the best meals are the ones that bring people closer. Sometimes that means a long lunch around a crowded table. Sometimes it means toast over the sink. Both count.

“Good food should feel generous, curious, and a little bit messy.”

What we believe

Flavor first.
People always.

Our recipes are tested in real home kitchens, our stories celebrate the people behind the plate, and our ordering menu is built for days when cooking simply isn’t happening.

We care about seasonal ingredients, clear instructions, and never making anybody feel like they don’t belong in the kitchen.

Who’s cooking

A small team with big appetites.

HS
Hapag Studio

Recipe makers, storytellers, lunch planners and enthusiastic dishwashers.

There’s room for you.
